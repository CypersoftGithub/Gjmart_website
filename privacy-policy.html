Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 01, 2025

1. Introduction

GjMart ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy and safeguarding your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect your information when you use our mobile application, website, or related services (collectively, the "Services"). By accessing or using the Services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of information:

Category Examples / Description
Personal Information You Provide Name, email address, phone number, delivery address, billing address, payment information, profile picture, customer support communications
Transactional Information Order history, products purchased, payment method, discounts or coupons used, returns, cancellations
Device & Usage Information IP address, device identifiers, operating system version, browser type, mobile network information, app version, crash logs
Location Data Approximate or precise location (with your permission) when you use location-based services (e.g. delivery, tracking)
Log & Analytics Data Time stamps, pages visited, clickstream data, referral source, user interactions, session duration
Cookies / Tracking Technologies Cookies, web beacons, SDKs, local storage to track preferences, sessions, analytics, and advertising

3. How We Use Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, maintain, and improve our Services
  • To process and fulfill your orders, payments, returns, refunds, cancellations
  • To communicate with you (order confirmations, delivery status, promotions, support)
  • To personalize your experience (recommendations, offers, content)
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, security risks, misuse, or violations
  • To comply with legal obligations, enforce our Terms & Conditions, resolve disputes
  • To analyze usage, trends, and customer behaviour for business optimization
  • To send marketing communications (with your consent or as permissible by law)

4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with:

  • Service providers & partners (e.g. payment processors, delivery/logistics partners, IT hosting, analytics, support)
  • Merchants / Vendors when necessary to fulfill your order, refunds, and returns
  • Legal / Regulatory Authorities in response to lawful requests or to protect rights, property, or safety
  • Business Transfers in connection with mergers, acquisitions, reorganization, or sale of assets
  • With your consent, in other scenarios

We ensure third parties are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for specified purposes.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described herein, unless a longer retention is required by law or permitted under applicable regulations. After that, we will delete, anonymize, or aggregate your data.

6. Security

We implement technical, administrative, and organizational measures to safeguard your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These may include encryption (in transit and at rest), access controls, regular audits, secure server environments, and minimizing data access to only those who need it.

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is fully secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights & Choices

You may have the following rights (as applicable under law):

  • Access, correct, update, or delete your personal information
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on your consent for processing
  • Opt out of marketing or promotional communications
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Portability: receive a copy of your data in machine-readable form
  • Lodge a complaint with relevant data protection authority

To exercise these rights, contact us via the contact details below. We may require identity verification before making changes.

8. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Maintain sessions, preferences, and login state
  • Analyze use of Services and generate analytics
  • Serve personalized advertisements or offers

You can typically disable or block cookies via browser or device settings. However, doing so may affect the functionality of the Services.

9. Children & Minors

Our Services are not intended for use by children under 18. If you are under 18, you may only use the Services under the supervision of a parent or legal guardian who agrees to these terms. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors without parental consent. If you believe we collected data from a minor, contact us to request deletion.

10. International Transfers

If your data is transferred outside your country (e.g. cross-border servers), we ensure that appropriate safeguards (e.g. standard contractual clauses, data transfer agreements) are in place to protect your information.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are significant, we will provide notice (e.g. via app notification, email, or prominent posting). Your continued use of the Services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
